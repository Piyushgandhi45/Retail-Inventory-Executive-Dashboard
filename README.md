# 📊 Retail & Inventory Analytics Executive Dashboard

An enterprise-grade, modern Business Intelligence dashboard built using **Power BI** to monitor retail performance, profitability dynamics, category spotlights, and customer behavior trends. Designed with a high-end corporate "floating card" aesthetic for recruitment and portfolio showcase.

---

## 🚀 Project Overview
In the fast-paced retail sector, management needs instant visibility into revenue drivers, product profitability, and regional demand patterns. This dashboard bridges the gap between raw transactional data and strategic decision-making by providing an interactive, deeply analytical command center.

---

## 📌 Key Business Metrics & KPIs
* **Total Revenue Tracked:** ₹624.77M
* **Overall Profit Margin:** 35% (Optimized via category-level margin tracking)
* **Total Profit Generated:** High-impact multi-category profitability breakdown
* **Total Orders Processed:** 19K+ transactions analyzed seamlessly

---

## 🎨 UI/UX & Design System
* **Aesthetic:** Corporate Slate theme featuring clean white floating containers (`#FFFFFF`), subtle drop shadows, and rounded borders (`6px radius`) over a modern light-gray canvas (`#F4F6F9`).
* **Typography:** Clean, uniform hierarchy utilizing `Segoe UI` for optimal enterprise readability.
* **Header Banner:** Custom-styled executive title banner ensuring immediate project context for stakeholders.

---

## 📊 Visual Breakdown & Analytical Features
1. **Executive KPI Cards:** Instant high-level health check of Sales, Profit Margin, Total Profit, and Orders.
2. **Monthly Sales Area Trend:** Smooth shaded area chart displaying revenue momentum and seasonal spikes across the year.
3. **Profit Breakdown by Category (Waterfall & Column):** Analyzes contribution bridges and exact margin percentages per product category.
4. **Payment Mode Distribution (Donut Chart):** Breaks down revenue share across UPI, Credit Card, Debit Card, Net Banking, and Cash on Delivery.
5. **Sales vs. Profit Trend (Combo Chart):** Dual-axis tracking comparing monthly top-line revenue against bottom-line profitability.
6. **Category Ranking & Trends:** Highlights shifting product performance over time.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Tools Used
* **Power BI Desktop:** Data modeling, DAX measures, interactive filtering, and layout design.
* **Data Modeling:** Star schema implementation with structured lookup and fact tables (`SalesData`, `DateTable`, `_Measures`).
* **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions):** Custom measures for dynamic aggregations, profit percentages, and financial formatting.
* **Dataset Type:** Secondary retail transactional dataset optimized for business intelligence portfolio demonstration.
* **Version Control:** GitHub for documentation and portfolio presentation.
